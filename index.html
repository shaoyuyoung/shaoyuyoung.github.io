About Me

I was born and raised in Nanjing, China. I’m an incoming Ph.D. student at Software Institue, Nanjing University, working with Associate Prof. Chunrong Fang. Previously, I obtained my M.Eng degree here. Before joining Nanjing University, I received my B.Eng degree from Nantong University, fortunately supervised by Associate Prof. Xiang Chen. My research interest lies at the intersection of AI Infras, Software Testing/Analysis, and Large Language Models,  aiming to build more reliable, efficient, and secure AI systems by designing LLM-integrated PL/SE techniques. I have disclosed 100+ previously unknown bugs for popular fundamental components of deep learning systems  (e.g., vLLM, SGLang, PyTorch, TensorFlow, TVM, Triton), including 14 CVEs. I’m also a contributor of PyTorch , aiming to fix some bugs in Inductor (a PyTorch native compiler).
